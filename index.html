Read a book and listen to it at the same time.

Entzun takes your ebook and its audiobook, lines them up word by word on your device, and lights up each word as the narrator reads it. For dyslexic readers, distracted readers, language learners, and anyone who reads better with a voice alongside.

Works with the books you already own, from any shop. No account, no server, nothing sent anywhere.

Who it is for

Built for people who read with their ears too.

If a page of text is hard going.

Hearing each word as you see it is one of the oldest ways to make reading stick. Dyslexic readers, readers with ADHD and readers with low vision have used paired text and audio for decades. Entzun does it with any book, at your speed, in the typeface and spacing that work for you.

If audio alone loses you.

Audiobooks are easy to drift out of. With the page in front of you, a wandering mind has somewhere to land, and a tap on any word takes the narrator back to it.

If you are reading in a second language.

See the spelling while you hear the pronunciation, slow the narrator to 1×, and tap a sentence as many times as you need. Nothing is looked up or sent anywhere.

Accessibility

Reading is not one thing, so the page is not one thing either.

Hearing the words is half of it. The rest of the reader is built so that the page itself can get out of the way.

How it works

Two files in, one book out.

1

Add both files

Pick the ebook, as EPUB or plain text, and the audiobook, as m4b, mp3 or m4a. Entzun keeps its own copy and offers to delete the originals.

2

It syncs on your device

The speech recogniser built into iOS and macOS transcribes the narration, and Entzun lines it up with the text word by word. A long book takes minutes, once, and you can start reading as soon as the first chapter is done.

3

Read and listen

Open the book and the narration starts where you left off. The word being spoken is marked, and the sentence being read stays in the middle of the page.

The reader

The page is the whole point.

Nothing sits over the sentence being read. The player floats well below it, and the controls can be hidden until it is just the book.

Hide controls

Everything but the book, gone.

One tap and the toolbar, the player and the clock leave the screen. The page stays, edge to edge, and the narration carries on.

Tap a word, hear it

Every word on the page is a place in the recording. Tap one and the narration jumps there; scroll away and a pill takes you back.

Silence when you want it

Pause the narration and read as you would any ebook. Start it again and it picks up from the sentence you are on.

iPhone and Mac

Native on each, with the same library and reader; on the Mac it can fill the screen. With iCloud, a book added on one is on the other, at the same page. How sync works.

Playback that fits the book

Skip fifteen seconds either way, choose a speed from 1× to 1.7×, and see the time played and left.

The library

A shelf that says what is going on.

Under every cover, one line of truth: how long the narration is, how much has synced, or, when the recording does not follow the text, a plain “Audio differs from text”.

Where to get books

Two files, and where to find them.

Entzun does not sell books. You bring the ebook and the audiobook, and they need to be files you can open, not ones locked to another app. That rules out Audible and Kindle purchases, which are protected. It leaves plenty:

  • Libro.fm and Downpour sell audiobooks as plain files you keep.
  • Project Gutenberg and Standard Ebooks have tens of thousands of out-of-copyright ebooks; LibriVox has free recordings of many of them. Quality varies, so look for a single-narrator reading.
  • Audiobook CDs you own rip to mp3 or m4a with the tools already on your Mac.
  • Ebooks from any shop that sells them without protection, and any EPUB you already have.

A studio recording of the same edition, one narrator, unabridged, gives the best result.

Price

One price. No account.

Entzun is free to download, with one book unlocked in full, so you can see your own files line up before you pay anything. Unlocking it for every book is 24.99 USD/EUR, once, for iPhone and Mac together, or 19.99 until 1 January 2027. Family Sharing covers everyone in your household.

Reading apps sold by subscription cost more than that every year. Entzun has no subscription, nothing to sign up for and nothing that stops working if a server goes away, because there is no server.

Privacy

Your books never leave your device. Neither does anything about you.

There are no accounts, no analytics, no crash reporter and no third-party code of any kind. Transcription happens on the device, with the speech recogniser Apple ships in the system. Entzun has no server and looks nothing up. The one place your books go is your own iCloud, if you are signed in, so that your other devices can have them; that is storage Apple runs for you, not for us.

That is the whole policy, more or less. The long version is short too.
